Privacy Policy

Last Updated:

1. Introduction

Welcome to HappyWeb ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and the privacy of your children. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard information when you use our mobile application HappyWeb (the "App").

This App is designed to provide a safe browsing experience for children under parental supervision. We take children's privacy seriously and comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and other applicable privacy laws.

2. Information We Collect

We collect nothing. HappyWeb does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information, browsing data, or usage statistics on external servers.

All data entered into the App (such as parent PIN, allowed/blocked websites, time limits, bedtime settings, and browsing history) is stored locally on your device only. This data never leaves your device and is never transmitted to us or any third parties.

We do not:

  • Collect personal information
  • Track browsing behavior
  • Gather usage statistics
  • Transmit data to external servers
  • Use analytics or tracking services
  • Share data with third parties
  • Store data in the cloud

3. Local Data Storage

The App stores the following information locally on your device only:

  • Parent PIN: Encrypted and stored locally
  • Allowed Websites: Stored locally on your device
  • Blocked Websites: Stored locally on your device
  • Time Limits: Stored locally on your device
  • Bedtime Settings: Stored locally on your device
  • Child Profile: Optional profile information stored locally
  • Browsing History: Stored locally and only visible to parents
  • Site Access Requests: Stored locally for parent review

Important: All of this data remains on your device. We have no access to it, and it is never transmitted to us or any external servers. When you uninstall the App, all local data is deleted.

4. Data Security

Since all data is stored locally on your device, the security of your data depends on the security of your device. We recommend:

  • Keeping your device password-protected
  • Keeping your parent PIN secure and confidential
  • Regularly updating your device's operating system

The App uses device-level encryption for sensitive data like the parent PIN, but we do not maintain any external security infrastructure since we don't collect or store any data.

5. Third-Party Services

The App uses WebKit (Apple's web rendering engine) to display web content. When your child visits a website through the App, that website's own privacy policy applies. We do not control the privacy practices of third-party websites.

If you buy the optional HappyWeb Premium subscription, the purchase is processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details, and we do not receive your name, email address, or Apple Account information. The App only learns from Apple whether a subscription is active on the device.

We do not share, sell, or rent any information to third parties because we don't collect any information in the first place. All data remains on your device.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

HappyWeb is designed for children under 13 years of age. We fully comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) because:

  • We collect no personal information from children - all data stays on the device
  • All settings and controls require parent authentication via PIN
  • Parents have full control over what websites their children can access
  • Parents can review and delete browsing history at any time
  • We do not use any tracking, analytics, or advertising services
  • We do not use persistent identifiers for any purpose

7. Parental Rights

As a parent, you have the right to:

  • Review all settings and restrictions at any time
  • Access and review your child's browsing history
  • Modify or delete allowed/blocked websites
  • Change time limits and bedtime settings
  • Delete all App data by uninstalling the App
  • Request information about what data is stored

8. Data Retention

Since we don't collect any data, there's nothing for us to retain. All data is stored locally on your device only. You can delete all local data at any time by:

  • Clearing browsing history through the App settings
  • Uninstalling the App (which removes all local data)

Once deleted from your device, the data is permanently gone. We have no copies because we never collected it in the first place.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.

10. Your Consent

By using HappyWeb, you consent to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use the App.
