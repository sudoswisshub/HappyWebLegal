Getting Started

First Time Setup

1 Create Your Parent PIN

When you first open HappyWeb, you'll be asked to create a 4-6 digit PIN. This PIN protects all parent settings and should be kept secure. Choose something memorable but not easily guessed by your child. Write it down somewhere your child can't find it, because a forgotten PIN can't be reset.

2 Create a Family Profile

Next, enter your child's name and age. You can also turn on the Site Library to start with a set of child-friendly sites. Tap "Finish Setup" when you're done. You can change the profile's avatar and color later.

3 Add Allowed Websites

Add websites that your child is allowed to visit. Tap the gear icon, enter your PIN, and tap "+" in the "Allowed Sites" section. You can add sites like educational platforms, approved games, or other safe content.

4 Configure Time Limits & Bedtime

Set daily time limits and bedtime hours (Bedtime Mode is part of HappyWeb Premium) to control when your child can use the browser. These settings help ensure healthy screen time habits.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How do I add a website to the allowed list?

A: To add a website:

  1. Tap the gear icon (⚙️) in the top right corner
  2. Enter your parent PIN
  3. Scroll to "Allowed Sites" and tap the "+" button
  4. Enter the website title and URL (e.g., "PBS Kids" and "pbskids.org")
  5. Tap "Add"
Q: What happens if my child tries to visit a blocked website?

A: If your child tries to visit a website that's not on the allowed list, they'll see a blocked page. They can request access to the site. The request appears in Settings under "Activity & Requests" > "Requests". You can then approve or deny the request.

Q: I forgot my parent PIN. How do I reset it?

A: HappyWeb 1.6 has no way to reset a forgotten PIN, so please keep it written down somewhere safe. If you have forgotten it, contact us before deleting the app. Deleting HappyWeb erases your profiles, allowed sites and history, and the PIN is kept in your device's secure storage, so reinstalling may not remove it.

Q: How do I change my parent PIN?

A: To change your PIN:

  1. Open Settings (gear icon) and enter your PIN
  2. Scroll to the "Security" section and tap "Change PIN"
  3. Enter your current PIN, then your new 4-6 digit PIN twice
  4. Tap "Update PIN"
Q: Can my child bypass the restrictions?

A: HappyWeb is designed with security in mind. All settings are protected by your parent PIN, and children cannot access or modify restrictions without it. However, no filtering system is 100% perfect, so we recommend active parental supervision alongside the app's controls.

Q: How do time limits work?

A: Time limits track daily usage for each profile. You can set a weekday limit from 15 minutes to 2 hours and a weekend limit from 30 minutes to 4 hours, or choose "No Limit" if you prefer to manage time manually. Homework Mode can add extra time, and Break Reminders can remind your child to take a break. When the limit is reached, your child sees a "Time's Up!" screen. From there they can tap "Ask for More Time", or you can use "Grown-Up Unlock" with your PIN to add extra minutes for today. The timer resets at midnight.

Q: What is Bedtime Mode?

A: Bedtime Mode automatically locks the browser during specified hours (e.g., 9 PM to 7 AM). When bedtime mode is active, your child will see a bedtime screen and cannot browse. If needed, a parent can tap "Emergency Unlock" and enter the PIN. Bedtime Mode is part of HappyWeb Premium.

Q: Can I see what websites my child has visited?

A: Yes, with HappyWeb Premium. In Settings, go to "Activity & Requests" and tap "Browsing History" to see the websites your child has visited. "Screen Time" in the same section shows how long your child browsed each day.

Q: Does HappyWeb collect or share my child's data?

A: No. HappyWeb does not collect, transmit, or store any data on external servers. All information (PIN, allowed sites, browsing history, etc.) is stored locally on your device only. We never see your data, and it never leaves your device. See our Privacy Policy for more details.

Q: What is "Word of the Day"?

A: Word of the Day is an optional educational feature that displays a new word each day on the home screen. You can enable or disable this feature in Parent Settings under "Learning".

Q: What is HappyWeb Premium?

A: Buying HappyWeb on the App Store gets you one child profile, allowed and blocked sites, daily time limits, SafeSearch, and site requests. HappyWeb Premium is an optional auto-renewing subscription (monthly or yearly) that adds a profile for every child, Bedtime Mode, Browsing History, and Site Collections. The yearly plan starts with a 3-day free trial for new subscribers. Families who installed HappyWeb before version 1.6 keep every feature at no extra cost.

Q: How do I subscribe, restore, or cancel Premium?

A: Open Settings with your parent PIN and tap the HappyWeb Premium banner to see the plans. If you already subscribed on this Apple Account, tap Restore Purchases on that screen. To cancel or change plans, open the App Store, tap your account, then Subscriptions, or use Manage Subscription in HappyWeb Settings. Cancelling stops the next renewal, and Premium stays active until the end of the period you paid for. To avoid being charged after a free trial, cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends. Purchases and refunds are handled by Apple.

Troubleshooting

Website Won't Load

  • Check your internet connection - Make sure your device is connected to Wi-Fi or cellular data
  • Verify the website is allowed - The site must be on your allowed list. Check if the exact domain matches (e.g., "www.example.com" vs "example.com")
  • Try adding the full URL - Some sites require the full URL including "https://"
  • Check if the site is blocked - Look in "Blocked Sites" to see if the domain was accidentally blocked

Settings Won't Save

  • Make sure you're entering your parent PIN correctly
  • Try closing and reopening the app
  • Restart your device if the problem persists
  • Ensure you have sufficient storage space on your device

Time Limit Not Working

  • Check the "Weekday Limit" and "Weekend Limit" for that profile in Settings under "Time Limits". "No Limit" turns the limit off
  • Make sure the right profile is selected. Each child profile has its own limits
  • Make sure your device's date and time are set correctly
  • The timer resets at midnight - if it's not resetting, check your device's time zone settings

Bedtime Mode Not Activating

  • Ensure "Enable Bedtime Mode" is turned on in Settings (Bedtime Mode is part of HappyWeb Premium)
  • Verify the start and end times are set correctly
  • Check that your device's date and time are accurate
  • Bedtime mode uses 24-hour format - make sure times are set correctly (e.g., 21:00 for 9 PM)

App Crashes or Freezes

  • Close the app completely and reopen it
  • Restart your device
  • Make sure you're running the latest version of the app
  • Check that your device has enough storage space
  • If the problem continues, contact us before uninstalling the app, because uninstalling deletes your profiles and settings

Features Guide

Browsing Modes

  • Whitelist Only: Only websites on your allowed list can be accessed. This is the most restrictive and safest option. Allowed pages can still show videos and games they embed from other sites, unless you add those sites to your blocked list.
  • Whitelist + Safe Browsing: Any website can open unless it is on your blocked list. With Risky Link Protection on, HappyWeb stops addresses that match known scam and malware patterns, but it does not judge what a page contains. Use it with SafeSearch and your blocked list, and only for children who are ready for the open web.

SafeSearch

When "Enforce SafeSearch" is on, HappyWeb turns on SafeSearch for searches on Google, Bing, and DuckDuckGo, which filters out explicit results. Other search engines are not covered, so block them if you don't want your child to use them.

Site Access Requests

When your child tries to visit a blocked site, they can request access. These requests appear in Settings under "Activity & Requests" > "Requests", where you can approve or deny them. Approved sites are automatically added to your allowed list.

Browsing History

With HappyWeb Premium, the websites your child visits are listed in the browsing history. You can review this history at any time in Settings. History is stored locally on your device and never shared with anyone.

Important: HappyWeb provides tools to help manage your child's internet access, but it is not a substitute for active parental supervision. We recommend regularly reviewing browsing history and having conversations with your child about internet safety.

Tips for Parents

  • Start with a small allowed list - Begin with a few trusted educational sites and gradually add more as needed
  • Review browsing history regularly - Check what your child has been viewing to ensure appropriate content
  • Use time limits wisely - Set reasonable limits based on your child's age and your family's values
  • Talk to your child - Explain why certain sites are blocked and discuss internet safety
  • Keep your PIN secure - Don't share your PIN with your child, and write it down in a safe place
  • Test the settings - Try accessing blocked sites yourself to verify restrictions are working
  • Update allowed sites regularly - As your child grows, you may want to add age-appropriate sites
